Suprapubic catheterization is an alternative to the Foley catheter. It can be used for both short- and long-term catheterization. However, in general, intermittent urethral catheterization is preferable to suprapubic catheterization. Insertion of the suprapubic catheter should be performed by a health professional with training and expertise in this procedure. Guidelines on insertion and care of this device have been published.
